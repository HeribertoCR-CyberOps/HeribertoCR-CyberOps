### What I do

I build and run **Savvy Solution Pros LLC** — a sovereign, on-premises managed security practice for small, high-stakes organizations such as NYC law and medical practices. The thesis is simple: security you actually **own**, data that never leaves your control, and evidence behind every claim.

- 🛡️ **Managed detection &amp; response** on a self-hosted stack — SIEM, agent telemetry, and hardening you can audit yourself.
- 📋 **Compliance engineering** — control mapping and audit-ready evidence, built against SOC 2-aligned controls.
- 🔐 **Privacy-by-design systems** — minimal collection, encryption at rest, nothing gathered that isn't needed.
- 🤖 **AI/LLM-assisted security tooling** — grounded, gated, and human-in-the-loop. Automation assists; people decide.

### Flagship open source — Walk With Us

[**Walk With Us**](https://github.com/HeribertoCR-CyberOps/walkwithus) is a free, privacy-first safe-passage platform for community organizations. It coordinates **trained adults** on corners at school times — and **never tracks children**. Runs on Cloudflare Workers + D1 at $0, AGPL-3.0.

> **Coordinates adults; never tracks children.** Free, forever.
